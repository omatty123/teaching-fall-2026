# Summary explainer sources

Dan Egan, *The Death and Life of the Great Lakes* (W. W. Norton, 2017).

- Chapter 3, "The World's Great Fishing Hole," pp. 75–107: introduced salmon as a response to the alewife invasion and as a sport fishery.
- Chapter 5, "Continental Undivide," pp. 151–186, especially 162–165 and 184: Chicago's canal connects separate watersheds and creates a possible invasion route. The explainer does not claim carp established a Great Lakes population.
- Chapter 7, "North America's 'Dead' Sea," pp. 212–244: earlier pollution controls and improvement, pp. 222–223; temporary recovery, p. 224; renewed bloom mechanisms, pp. 227–231; Toledo drinking-water crisis, pp. 238–241.
