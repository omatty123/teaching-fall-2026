Pound and guinea

The pound is a unit of money: 20 shillings. The guinea is a gold coin. It originally represented one pound, but its value changed as gold’s price shifted against silver. In 1717, its value was set at 21 shillings: one pound plus one shilling, or 5% more. 1717 proclamation The pictured one-pound sovereign came in 1817, after the book. The pictured penny is a later 1797 issue. Royal Mint Museum

Halfpenny: ½ penny. Farthing: ¼ penny. Pence means pennies. British values

Reading the symbols

£1 10s 6d means 1 pound, 10 shillings and 6 pennies.

d comes from denarius, an ancient Roman coin. Britain kept the Latin abbreviation: 1d = one penny; 12d = twelve pence. Royal Mint Museum
