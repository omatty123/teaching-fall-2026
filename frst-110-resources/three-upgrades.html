Three upgrades for your Synthesis revision

1Lead with your point.Your real point is often in your conclusion. Start with it.

YOUR OPENING“Disaster is inevitable.”
→
YOUR REAL POINT, FROM YOUR CONCLUSION“The impact of the Great Lakes invasive species and the Hurricane Katrina aftermath could have been lessened had there been proper planning and action.”

2Cut the throat-clearing.The words you say before you get to your point.

BEFOREThis quote demonstrates the effort and persistence that was expressed by Noah to keep his people safe but also their unwillingness to listen.
→
AFTERNoah tried, but nobody listened.

3Quote exactly.One missing word changes the meaning.

THE PAPER“Anything I need to do or tweak?”
→
SMITH, P. 28“Anything specific I need to do / or tweak?”
