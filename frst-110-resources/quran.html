Nuh in the Quran

Nuh is the Arabic name for Noah. The assigned passages are Sura 71 and Sura 11:25–49, translated by Alan Jones.

  1. Sura 71 Noah’s preaching, his people’s refusal, and his prayer.
  2. Sura 11 Noah’s dispute with the leaders, the ship, the flood, and his son.

Sura 71 · Noah

Packet note

Sura 71 is named for Noah. It is almost all his preaching: night and day, in public and in secret. The Flood itself takes one verse.

About this text

Translator’s note on this sura (Alan Jones): “On the surface the sūra deals simply with the story of Noah, and one should certainly first look at it in this way. However, it is of composite nature, and it seems unlikely that verse 23, which includes the names of five pre-Islamic gods, could have been part of any original narrative. The present structure is complex. The main narrative basically comprises verses 1–4 and 25, but there are further narratives taking up verses 5–12, 15–20 and 21–24. One might have expected verses 26–28, a prayer from Noah, to precede 25, but they do not.”

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِIn the name of the Merciful and Compassionate God

71:1We sent Noah to his people, saying,
‘Warn yourˢ people before a painful punishment comes upon them.’
Term

Who is “We”?

God, speaking. The Quran often has God speak as “We,” the way a king says “we.” It does not mean more than one god.

Term

The little ˢ and ᵖ

The translator’s marks. English “you” can mean one person or many; Arabic says which. ˢ means you (one person), ᵖ means you (all of you). Here God speaks to Noah alone.

71:2He said, ‘O my people,
I am a clear warner to you.
Term

A warner

Noah warns his people before the punishment comes. The word returns in 11:25.

71:3“Serve God and fear Him and obey me,
71:4And He will forgive you some of your sins
and defer you to a stated term.
God’s term, when it comes, cannot be deferred
if you did but know it”.’
71:5He said, ‘My Lord,
I have summoned my people night and day,
71:6But my summoning has increased them only in flight.
71:7Whenever I summon them so that You might forgive them,
they put their fingers in their ears
and draw their garments over them
and persist and are full of pride.
71:8I have summoned them publicly.
71:9I spoke to them openly
and I spoke to them secretly
71:10And I said,
“Ask forgiveness of yourᵖ Lord
– He is Forgiving

Rain as a reward

The translator’s footnote: “With abundant rain.” Here heavy rain is a blessing for people who turn back to God, not the Flood.

71:12And support you with wealth and sons,
and will assign gardens to you,
and will assign rivers to you.
71:13What is the matter with you
that you do not expect God to have dignity,
71:14When he has created you in stages?

Seven heavens

The sky built in seven layers, one above another.

71:16And made the moon a light among them and made the sun a lamp?
71:17It is God who has caused you to spring up as a growth from the earth.
71:18Then He will make you return to it,
and He will bring you forth [again].
71:19And God has made the earth a carpet for you,
71:20That you might thread ways through its valleys”.’
71:21Noah said,
‘My Lord, they have rebelled against me
and followed him whose wealth and children
increase him only in loss,
Person

Who is “him”?

Not a named person: whoever is rich and powerful. Noah’s people follow the wealthy leaders instead of him.

71:22And they have plotted a very great plot,
71:23And said,
“Do not forsake your gods
and do not forsake Wadd nor Suwāʿ
nor Yaghūth nor Yaʿūq nor Nasr.”
Person

Five gods

The translator’s footnote: “The names are those of some of the deities worshipped by pre-Islamic Arabs,” the gods of Arabia before Islam. He also argues this verse was probably not part of the original story (see the note on the sura’s heading).

71:24And they have led many astray.
Do not increase the wrong-doers except in error.’
71:25Because of their sins they were drowned
and put into a fire;
and they found no helpers apart from God.
Term

Drowned, then fire

Drowning ends their lives; the fire is punishment after death. Sura 71 describes the drowning in verse 25.

71:26Noah said, ‘O my Lord,
do not leave a single one of the unbelievers on the earth.
71:27If You leave them, they will lead Your servants astray,
and beget only dissolute unbelievers.
71:28My Lord,
forgive me and my parents
and whoever enters my house as a believer
and believing men and women,
and increase the wrong-doers only in ruin.’

Sura 11 · Hud (verses 25–49)

Packet note

Sura 11:25–49 recounts Noah’s dispute with the leaders, the ship, his son’s refusal to board, and the landing.

11:25We sent Noah to his people,
‘I am a clear warner for you,
11:26That you should serve only God.
I fear for you the punishment of a painful day.’
11:27The notables of his people,
p. 210
who disbelieved, said,
‘We do not see you to be anything other than a human being like us.
We do not see that you are followed by any other than the vilest of us,
without consideration.
We do not see that you have any superiority over us.
On the contrary, we think you liars.’
Person

The notables

The leading men of the town: the rich and respected. They are the ones who argue with Noah and mock him.

Term

“The vilest of us”

The lowest people in town. The notables sneer that only the poor and unimportant follow Noah.

11:28He said, ‘O my people, have you considered:
if I stand on a clear proof from my Lord
and [if] He has given me mercy from Him
and it has been obscured from your sight,
can we compel you to accept it,
when you are averse?
11:29My people,
I am not asking you for any money for this.
My reward is only with God.
I shall not drive away those who believe.
They will meet their Lord.
But I see that you are an ignorant people.
11:30My people,
who would help me against God if I drive them away?
Will you not be reminded?
11:31I do not say to you
that the treasuries of God are with me
nor that I have knowledge of the Invisible;
nor do I say that I am an angel;
nor do I say to those whom your eyes scorn
that God will not give them good
(God knows best what is in their hearts).
If I were to do that,
I should be one of the wrong-doers.’
Term

The Invisible

Everything hidden from human knowledge, known only to God: the future, the unseen world. Noah claims no special powers.

11:32They said, ‘O Noah, you have disputed with us
– and disputed much with us.
Bring to us what you promise us,
if you are truthful.’
11:33He said, ‘Only God can bring it to you if He wishes,
and you will not be able to frustrate [it].
11:34My advice would not benefit you,
[even] if I want to give you good advice,
if God wishes to lead you astray.
He is your Lord and to Him you will be returned.’
11:35Or do they say, ‘He has invented it’?
Say, ‘If I invent it, my sin is upon me,

but I am free of the sins that you commit.’
Claim

Who is speaking now?

The story pauses. “Say” is addressed to the Prophet Muhammad, about people in his own time who called the Quran made up. Then the Noah story resumes.

11:36It was suggested to Noah,
‘None of your people will believe,
unless they are already believers.
Do not be distressed at what they have been doing.
11:37Make the ship under Our eyes and at Our inspiration.
Do not address me concerning those who do wrong.
They will be drowned.’
11:38He [went about] making the ship,
and every time that some notables from his people passed by him,
they mocked him.
He said, ‘If you mock us, we shall mock you even as you mock.
11:39You will know to whom comes a punishment that will shame him
and on whom descends a lasting punishment.’
11:40Then, when Our command came and the oven boiled,
We said, ‘Load into it two of every kind and your family
– except for those against whom the word has already gone forth –
and those who believe.’
And there were only a few who had believed with him.
Term

The oven boiled

The translator’s footnote: the phrase “appears to echo a post-Biblical Jewish idea that the waters of the flood were hot.”

Claim

Already judged

People God has already condemned are left out, even from Noah’s family. Keep this line in mind at verse 43.

11:41He said, ‘Embark in it.
In God’s name will be its course and its anchorage.
My Lord is Forgiving and Merciful.’
Term

Course and anchorage

Its sailing and its coming to rest: the whole voyage, start to finish, in God’s name.

11:42It sailed with them among waves like mountains,
and Noah called to his son,
who was in an isolated place,
‘My son, embark with us and do not be with the unbelievers.’
11:43He said, ‘I shall go and seek refuge on a mountain
which will protect me from the water.’
He said, ‘To-day there is no protector from God’s command,
except for the one on whom He has mercy.’
And the waves came between the two of them,
and he was among those drowned.
Person

“He” is the son

The translator’s footnotes: this “He” is Noah’s son; the next “He said” is Noah.

11:44[Then] it was said, ‘O earth, swallow your water; O sky, abate.’
The water subsided, and the affair was accomplished.
[The ship] settled on al-Jūdī,
p. 212
and it was said, ‘Away with the wrong-doers.’
Place

Al-Jūdī

The translator’s footnote: “Jewish and later Muslim tradition places al-Jūdī in present day Kurdistan, but initially Arab hearers would have identified it with a mountain in northern central Arabia.”

11:45Noah cried out to his Lord, saying,
‘My Lord, my son is one of my family.
Your promise is the truth;
You are the wisest of those who judge.’
11:46He said, ‘O Noah, he is not one of your family.
It would be an unrighteous deed.
Do not ask me for that of which you have no knowledge.
I admonish you not to be one of the ignorant.’
Term

An unclear line

The Arabic here can be read two ways: that asking for the son is not right, or that the son’s own conduct was not righteous. Translators differ.

11:47He said, ‘My Lord, I seek refuge in You from asking You
for that of which I have no knowledge.
If You do not forgive me and do not have mercy on me
I shall be one of the losers.’
11:48It was said, ‘O Noah, descend with peace from Us
and with blessings on you
and on communities [that spring] from those who are with you.
And there will be [other] communities to whom We shall give enjoyment,
but then a painful punishment from Us will touch them.’
11:49That is of the tidings of the Invisible,
which We impart to you.
You did not know it yourself nor did your people before this.
Have patience.
The end will be in favour of those who are god-fearing.’
Claim

Who is “you” now?

The Prophet Muhammad again. The story ends by speaking to him: this is news he did not know, a lesson in patience.

Discussion: disaster and responsibility

Water makes life possible, but we cannot fully control it. How do people live with that dependence and vulnerability?

  1. Does suffering have to mean someone did something wrong?

    What happens to our understanding of the victims when a story explains disaster as punishment?

    Quran 71:25–28 · Genesis 6:5–13

  2. Why do people sometimes know, but not care?

    Noah warns his people again and again. What besides information would have to change for them to listen? Compare their response with one example from Egan.

    Quran 71:5–9

  3. What happens when people care, but don’t know what to do?

    What turns concern into useful action? Use one example from Egan to explain what someone would need to learn, and whose help they would need.

  4. What do people owe one another when survival is unequal?

    Is saving your own family enough? What about strangers and other living things?

    Quran 11:40–46 · Genesis 9:8–17

How do we get people to know, to care, and to keep doing something? For the Great Lakes, what could sustain that responsibility over a lifetime and across generations? Explain how a habit, a community, or an institution could keep the work going after the immediate danger has passed.
