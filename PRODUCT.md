# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript generated by `python3 build.py` and published with GitHub Pages. No framework or build server.

## Users

- Matty Wegehaupt, using the instructor HQ frequently to orient to the teaching day, see the next consequential action, and open the correct course or private system.
- Fall 2026 students, using the separate public course index and course pages to find the next meeting, Canvas, syllabus, current work, and schedule without encountering instructor workflow.

## Product Purpose

The system turns three distinct course projects into two trustworthy launch surfaces: a compact instructor operating desk and a student-forward public course doorway. Success means each visitor can identify what matters now and reach the correct destination in seconds, while repeated public facts remain synchronized with their owning course sources.

## Positioning

This is not a generic LMS dashboard. It is an evidence-aware teaching field desk organized around the actual rhythm of one term: meetings, current questions, consequential decisions, source provenance, and the next action before class.

## Operating Context

- The instructor HQ is checked before and between teaching days.
- Google Docs syllabi and Canvas remain authoritative external student systems where applicable.
- `data/term.json` and `data/<slug>.json` own the public projection and generate every published HTML page.
- Private course notes and Access-gated roster tools remain outside the public artifact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing static generator, course schedules, working links, course colors, documentary images, and browser-local preparation checklists.
- The public student index must not include private roster links, instructor-only commentary, unpublished student information, or local filesystem destinations.
- Unresolved academic facts remain explicit and must not be silently promoted into confident public claims.
- FRST meets Monday, Wednesday, and Friday; HIST meets Tuesday and Thursday; BUEN meets Tuesday only.
- Motion is minimal and communicates state only.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Fall 2026 Teaching HQ.
- The user selected the “Field Desk” direction: compact, information-rich, distinctly authored, and consistent without looking templated or AI-generated.
- Use sans-serif typography and documentary images that are directly related to a source, place, work, or idea.

## Evidence on Hand

- Structured term and course data under `data/`.
- Current course schedules, meeting metadata, destinations, documentary banners, and FRST works imagery.
- The existing Water in the News page is the strongest incumbent expression of the Field Desk idea.
- Private Fall 2026 course `STATUS.md` files document authoritative decisions and unresolved conflicts; they are not public website data sources.

## Product Principles

1. Show the next consequential thing before the complete inventory.
2. Separate audiences structurally, not with labels alone.
3. Evidence and provenance precede decoration.
4. Rows, rules, and comparison outrank generic cards.
5. Unknown, stale, private, and unavailable are distinct states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast and interaction, use at least 44-by-44-pixel practical touch targets, retain visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support, and keep student navigation understandable without familiarity with instructor terminology.
