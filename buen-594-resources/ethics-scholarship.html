Scholarship Application

Published case · Kenan Institute for Ethics, Duke University

You are interning abroad with an organization that helps students apply for scholarships. An application is due tomorrow. Your supervisor tells you to copy a withdrawn applicant’s work into another student’s application. You say you are uncomfortable. Your supervisor says this is how it is done here and wants it finished.

As a COMPASS

ethics guide you

Helping a student get her chance, honest authorship, and fairness to the other applicants.

As a CONUNDRUM

ethics implicate you

Help can turn into misrepresentation. Improving her writing is one thing. Handing in someone else’s work under her name is another.

As a CRUCIBLE

ethics test you

  • Body A deadline tomorrow and a late night.
  • Emotions Anxiety, and your supervisor’s growing displeasure.
  • Conscience Honest work against getting her the scholarship.
  • Relationships Your supervisor’s authority, local custom, and the two fellow interns you turn to.
Where the case leaves us

The interns, Chris among them, see two paths: find the applicant and help her rewrite tonight, or polish her draft without her. The case leaves the choice open.
