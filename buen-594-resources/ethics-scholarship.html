Scholarship Application

Published teaching case · adapted student experience

An NGO supervisor tells intern Chris to copy a withdrawn applicant’s work into another student’s scholarship application. It is due tomorrow. Chris objects, but the supervisor says the practice is locally accepted and wants it finished.

COMPASS

Guide you

Support an applicant’s opportunity while respecting truthful authorship and fair treatment of other applicants.

CONUNDRUM

Implicate you

Helping can become misrepresentation. Chris must distinguish improving someone’s writing from submitting another person’s work as theirs.

CRUCIBLE

Test you

Possible pressures—not reported inner experiences.

  • Physical Tomorrow’s deadline limits time to find the applicant and help.
  • Emotional Your own anxiety; the supervisor’s displeasure.
  • Spiritual / moral Your conscience: truthful work alongside helping someone gain access.
  • Social Supervisor → intern; local expectations → newcomer; fellow interns → support.

The three Cs offer an interpretation for discussion.

Where the case leaves us

Chris and the other interns consider finding the applicant and helping her rewrite. The case does not supply a final resolution.
