Examine the situation with CLEAR

  1. CCheck facts

    Separate evidence from assumptions. Identify what you still need to know.

  2. LListen to people

    Notice feelings, including your own. Check interpretations and differences in power.

  3. EExamine obligations

    Consider honesty, fairness, care, rights, and commitments. Repeated choices also shape character.

  4. AAnticipate outcomes

    Consider immediate and later effects, alternatives, and uncertainty.

  5. RRespond and review

    Act with reasons and support. Observe the results, then revise or repair.

        About CLEAR and these simulations

        CLEAR is a course mnemonic created for these exercises. It combines ethical scrutiny with emotional awareness and attention to possible consequences. It is not a validated assessment or a method for assigning an ethics score.

        Background: Markkula Center’s framework for ethical decision-making and RULER’s overview of emotional-intelligence skills. The scenarios, choices, and outcome paths are original teaching fiction.
