Full Disclosure

Published case · Ethics Unwrapped, University of Texas at Austin

You are interning in the fundraising office of a shelter for Central American immigrants. At a dinner, a donor writes a large check, believing the shelter serves U.S. citizens. Your fundraising director lets him believe it. You know the gift rests on a misunderstanding.

As a COMPASS

ethics guide you

Honesty with donors, and care for the people the shelter serves. How an organization raises money says what kind of organization it is.

As a CONUNDRUM

ethics implicate you

You saw it, so you are already in it. Stay quiet and the donor stays wrong. Speak up and you may cost the shelter money and strain things with your director.

As a CRUCIBLE

ethics test you

  • Body Late in the evening, with no time to think it over.
  • Emotions Unease, and fear of embarrassing your director.
  • Conscience Honesty with the donor against what the money means for the residents.
  • Relationships Your director has power over your internship. The donor has power over the budget.
Where the case leaves us

The case does not say what Jenny, the intern, does. Whether to correct the donor and how to raise it are two separate choices.
