Full Disclosure

Published teaching case · adapted student experience

Intern Jenny watches a donor give a large check to a shelter serving Central American immigrants. He believes it serves U.S. citizens. Her fundraising director does not correct him. Jenny knows the donation rests on a misunderstanding.

As a COMPASS

ethics guide you

Honesty and care for people in need both matter. Fundraising methods help define the organization’s character.

As a CONUNDRUM

ethics implicate you

Jenny is already involved as a witness. Silence leaves the donor mistaken; speaking could threaten funding and her relationship with her supervisor.

As a CRUCIBLE

ethics test you

Possible pressures—not reported inner experiences.

  • Physical Tiredness late in the evening; little time to collect yourself.
  • Emotional Your own unease; fear of embarrassing your supervisor.
  • Spiritual / moral Your conscience: honesty alongside care for shelter residents.
  • Social Supervisor → intern; donor → organization; residents’ needs → staff.

The three Cs offer an interpretation for discussion.

Where the case leaves us

The published case leaves Jenny’s next action open. Correcting the misunderstanding and choosing how to raise it are separate decisions.
