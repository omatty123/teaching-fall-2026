The Coaching Intern

Imagined case · youth basketball

You are interning with a youth basketball program. All season you have told your players that everyone gets fair playing time. Now, in close games, the head coach plays only the starters, and the kids you coached sit on the bench. What do you do?

As a COMPASS

ethics guide you

Fairness to every player, your word to the kids, and respect for the head coach who runs the team.

As a CONUNDRUM

ethics implicate you

You made a promise the head coach never made. Stay quiet and you break your word to the players. Push back and you strain the relationship your internship depends on. A win-win may still be there: more minutes in practice, and in games already decided.

As a CRUCIBLE

ethics test you

  • Body Long practices and game nights on top of your own classes.
  • Emotions Guilt in front of kids you encouraged, and fear of looking disloyal.
  • Conscience Your word to the kids against the head coach’s authority.
  • Relationships The head coach expects loyalty. The players remember what you told them. Parents may ask you why their kids sit.
