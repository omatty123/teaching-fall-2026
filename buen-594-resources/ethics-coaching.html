The Coaching Intern

Imagined case · youth basketball

You are interning with a youth basketball program. All season you have told your players that everyone gets fair playing time. Now, in close games, the head coach plays only the starters, and the kids you coached sit on the bench. What do you do?

As a COMPASS

ethics guide you

Fairness to every player, keeping your word, and respect for the head coach who runs the team.

As a CONUNDRUM

ethics implicate you

You made a promise the head coach never made. Staying quiet breaks your word to the players. Pushing back strains the relationship your internship depends on. A win-win may still be there: more reps in practice, and in games already decided.

As a CRUCIBLE

ethics test you

  • Physical Long practices and game nights on top of your own classes.
  • Emotional Guilt in front of kids you encouraged; fear of looking disloyal.
  • Spiritual / moral Your word against your supervisor’s authority.
  • Social Head coach → you; you → players; parents → you.
