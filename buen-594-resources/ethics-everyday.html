Everyday Conundrums

BUEN 594 · Ethics panel
Tuesday, November 10 · 12:40 p.m.
Fox Commons, Business and Entrepreneurship Center

None of these is a scandal. Each is an ordinary moment at work where loyalty, generosity, discretion, fairness, and ambition can pull against each other.

  1. Hiring a friendYou pick someone you know over a stronger applicant because you trust them and want to help.
  2. Inside knowledgeYou act on information that is legal to use but that your profession expects you to keep confidential.
  3. A head startYou tell a friend about an opening before anyone else hears of it.
  4. Interview helpYou coach a candidate when you already know the exact questions they will face.
  5. Taking creditYour supervisor praises you for work a quieter colleague mostly did, and you let it stand.
  6. Covering for someoneYou hide a coworker’s mistake because they once covered for you.
  7. Better numbersYou report the favorable results and leave out the inconvenient ones.
  8. Promising too muchYou assure a client your team can deliver before asking the people who will do the work.
  9. Making an exceptionYou extend a deadline for someone whose situation you understand, and hold everyone else to the original deadline.
  10. Accepting a favorYou take tickets or a dinner from someone whose proposal you will later judge.
  11. Passing work downYou hand an intern the tasks you dislike and call it a learning opportunity.
  12. Always onYou answer messages late at night to help your team and set an expectation others cannot meet.
  13. Staying silentYou let a demeaning joke pass because the person telling it controls your next opportunity.
  14. A generous recommendationYou overstate someone’s abilities because you care about them and they need the job.
  15. Someone else’s storyYou tell a vivid workplace story that makes a colleague recognizable without naming them.
  16. Leaving at a hard timeYou take a better offer, knowing your departure will burden colleagues who backed you.
