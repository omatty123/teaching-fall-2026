Words to say

When you have little power, the skill is finding words that let the person who can act want to act. Seven moves, with sentences you can borrow.

Buy time“Can I think about that and get back to you this afternoon?”
Ask, don’t accuse“Help me understand how this works here. Is this how we usually handle it?”
Name the risk to them“I’m worried that if the donor finds out later, it could cost us more than this check.”
Offer a better path“What if I help her rewrite her own application tonight? It stays her work, and it still goes in on time.”
Translate ethics into their interest“Repeat customers keep us going. I’m not sure this pricing helps us with them.”
Keep your word without a fight“I told the kids everyone would play. Could the bench get minutes once the game is out of reach?”
Find an ally first“Before I raise this, would you look at it with me?”
