Tue Sep 15Geography and prehistory

關雎Guanju

Shijing (Classic of Poetry), Mao no. 1

Poem: Zhou dynasty, first millennium BCE. Image: later illustrated album leaf pairing the text with a river scene.

Guan ju · Wikipediaimage source · Public domain

  1. ElementalWho is in it, where, and what happens?
  2. IntermediateFour stanzas, one plant, three verbs. What does the repetition do?
  3. AdvancedHow does a love song become a lesson in statecraft?

Thu Sep 17Archaeology and writing

三星堆青銅大立人Standing figure

Sanxingdui, Guanghan, Sichuan

Bronze, c. 1200 BCE (late Shang period). Excavated 1986, Sanxingdui pit 2. Sanxingdui Museum.

Sanxingdui · Wikipediaimage source · CC0

  1. ElementalDescribe it without naming it. Hands, eyes, feet, scale.
  2. IntermediateCompare those hands to a Shang ritual vessel. What is each object for?
  3. AdvancedShang left records; Sanxingdui did not. What does that do to the history?

Tue Sep 22Mandate of Heaven, House of Ru

大盂鼎Da Yu ding

Western Zhou ritual cauldron

Bronze, reign of King Kang, c. 11th century BCE. Inscription of 291 characters. Excavated Qishan, Shaanxi, 19th century. National Museum of China, Beijing.

Da Yu ding · Wikipediaimage source · CC BY-SA 4.0

  1. ElementalWhere is the writing, and who could ever see it?
  2. IntermediateIt cooks offerings for the dead. How does that change the claim?
  3. AdvancedWho is a Mandate claim addressed to?

Thu Sep 24House of Mo challenges the Ru

曾侯乙編鐘Bells of Marquis Yi of Zeng

Sixty-five bronze bells from one tomb

Bronze, buried 433 BCE. Excavated 1978, Leigudun, Suizhou, Hubei. Hubei Provincial Museum.

Bianzhong of Marquis Yi · Wikipediaimage source · CC BY-SA 2.0

  1. ElementalCount what you see. What did it cost to make?
  2. IntermediateEach bell sounds two pitches. What knowledge is behind that?
  3. AdvancedDoes the object prove Mozi right or prove him a philistine?

Tue Sep 29House of Dao; Ru 2.0

人物御龍帛畫Man Riding a Dragon

Painted silk banner from a Chu tomb

Man riding a dragon · Wikipediaimage source · Public domain

  1. ElementalWhat is the man doing, and where is he going?
  2. IntermediateIt was folded into a coffin. What is a painting for if its viewer is dead?
  3. AdvancedRiding the six vapours: metaphor, claim, or instructions?

Thu Oct 1Ru 3.0 and the House of Fa

Tue Oct 20馬大王之清談 · The Great Debate

步輦圖Emperor Taizong Receives the Envoy

Attributed to Yan Liben

Handscroll, ink and colour on silk. Attributed to Yan Liben (c. 600–673); the surviving scroll is generally taken to be a later Song copy. Palace Museum, Beijing.

Bunian tu · Wikipediaimage source · Public domain

  1. ElementalWho is seated, who stands, and how do you know the rank?
  2. IntermediateThe emperor is borne by women with fans and screens. What is that doing?
  3. AdvancedYour house has one audience with the throne. What does this scene say about how to use it?

Tue Oct 27Song: cities, gender, Zhu Xi

清明上河圖Along the River During the Qingming Festival

Zhang Zeduan · detail of the original scroll

Handscroll, ink and colour on silk, Northern Song, early 12th century. 25.5 × 525 cm. Palace Museum, Beijing.

Qingming shanghe tu · Wikipediaimage source · Public domain

  1. ElementalPick one square inch. Who is there and what do they do for a living?
  2. IntermediateNo frame, no vanishing point. How does a handscroll make you read a city?
  3. AdvancedZhu Xi wants granaries and family ritual. Is he describing this society or arguing with it?
