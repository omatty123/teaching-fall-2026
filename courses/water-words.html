    Why the words cluster

    Related languages descend from a common ancestor, and everyday words come along. English water, German Wasser, Russian voda and Lithuanian vanduo all go back to Proto-Indo-European *wódr̥. Latin aqua became eau, agua, acqua, apă.

    Families also trade words in. Hindi pānī comes from Sanskrit pānīya, “drinkable.” Tamil taṇṇīr is literally “cold water.” Malagasy rano is kin to Indonesian danau, “lake.”

    Nibi is Ojibwe (Anishinaabemowin), a language of the Great Lakes, spoken here long before English.
