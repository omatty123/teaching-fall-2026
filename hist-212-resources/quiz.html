Notes on dates and names

All ancient dates here are BCE unless marked otherwise. Larger BCE numbers are earlier. Historical BCE/CE notation has no year 0.

Xia’s dates and its identification with Erlitou remain uncertain. Laozi’s identity and dates are disputed; several other thinkers’ lifespans are approximate. You are not asked to memorize uncertain birth years.

771 BCE marks the fall of Western Zhou; 770 BCE conventionally begins Eastern Zhou. King You was the last Western Zhou king, not the last king of all Zhou. King Zhòu 紂 of Shang is distinct from the Zhōu 周 dynasty.

The books developed over time: their placement on a timeline does not identify a single composition date. Fengjing’s spelling 灃京 is a variant; 豐京 is used here. Chengzhou, Wangcheng, and Luoyi belong to a complex capital history around Luoyang.
