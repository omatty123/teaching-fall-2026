Submit in Canvas: Question and evidence

Two passages from Salt and Iron count as one primary source for the final project.

Final project: two periods, three East Asian societies, four primary sources. To connect past and present, trace transmission or revival; resemblance alone does not prove inheritance.

A proposal shows what its author wanted. To establish influence, add evidence of adoption, adaptation, rejection, or later revival. Separate evidence from the period from later claims about it.

Source locations and next steps

SCT: Sources of Chinese Tradition. SKT: Sources of Korean Tradition. SJT: Sources of Japanese Tradition. CC: Chinese Civilization: A Sourcebook. Use the assigned editions in the course readers.

For Salt and Iron, compare the minister and learned men on CC 61. For Qin’s legacy, add Lu Jia in the Han philosophy packet. To investigate later Confucian influence, start with Zhu Xi’s academy rules, SCT 743, and ancestral rites, CC 157–163. For present-day influence, identify a specific practice or claim and document its history; do not assume the modern resemblance proves descent.
