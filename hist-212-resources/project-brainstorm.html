Politics

Why wasn’t there another Qin—or was there?

Li Si’s two memorials (SCT 207–212): centralized rule. Lu Jia, opening of the Han philosophy packet: lessons from Qin’s failure.

Politics

Do people behave better because of virtue or punishment?

Kongzi, Analects 2.3: virtue and shame. Han Feizi, ch. 7, “The Two Handles” (p. 333): punishment and reward.

Culture

What ancient ideas still live on now?

Start with family duty: Analects 1.2 and Wŏn’gwang’s Five Commandments (SKT 45–46). Compare what each asks people to do.

Culture

Should religious people obey rulers?

Huiyuan, “A Monk Does Not Bow Down Before a King” (SCT 427–429); Wŏn’gwang’s response to the war-petition request (SKT 45).

War

Can a war be just?

Mozi, ch. 17 (p. 73): attacking another state. Salt and Iron (CC 61): the minister’s defense of frontier military spending.

War

Is a strong army the same as a strong society?

Lord Shang, 3.2 (p. 134): agriculture and warfare. Mozi, ch. 17 (p. 73): the harm of aggressive war.

Economics

Should the government run the economy?

Salt and Iron (CC 61): compare the minister’s defense of monopolies with the learned men’s objections. Two passages; one source.

Economics

Would people be better off if rulers did less?

Laozi 37 (p. 189): a world that orders itself. Taika reform edicts (SJT 77–79): registers, land, and limits on powerful households.

Finish the homework

  1. State your question. Choose one above, adapt it, or propose your own.
  2. Give your tentative answer. Write one or two sentences that answer the question. You can change your answer after reading.
  3. Present and explain passage 1. Copy a short quotation and give its source and page/section. Identify who is speaking and the situation. Explain what the words show about your question.
  4. Present and explain passage 2. Do the same for a second passage. Explain how it supports, challenges, or changes your answer.

Before you submit: check both quotations and citations. Explain the evidence; do not just paste it. Submit about one page through the Question and evidence assignment in Canvas.

Later, for the final project: expand to two periods, three East Asian societies, and four primary sources, with an evidenced connection to the present. You do not need that complete comparison in this first submission.

Reading abbreviations and scope

CC: Chinese Civilization: A Sourcebook. SCT: Sources of Chinese Tradition. SKT: Sources of Korean Tradition. SJT: Sources of Japanese Tradition. Other page numbers refer to the assigned course packets. These are starting passages, not complete research plans. For the ancient-ideas question, a claim about survival into the present needs further evidence. The two positions in Salt and Iron belong to one primary source.
